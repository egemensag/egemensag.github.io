Frequently asked questions

How does the combo work?

Every row or column you clear adds to your combo. You can make a couple of non-clearing “preparation” moves without losing it (two moves below x7, one move at x7 and above). Keep clearing to reach Flow, Fever and Overdrive.

What do Board Rescue and Undo do?

Board Rescue (once per game) clears a few cells when you run out of moves, guaranteeing a line-clearing move. Undo (once per game) reverses your last placement. Both are optional and unlocked by watching a short ad. If the ad doesn’t finish, you keep your Rescue or Undo.

What does “Remove Ads” include?

It permanently removes the automatic ads shown between games. Optional reward ads for Board Rescue and Undo remain available, because you choose when to watch them.

I bought Remove Ads on another device or reinstalled the game.

Open Settings → Remove Ads → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

My score doesn’t appear on the leaderboard.

Make sure you are signed in to Game Center (iPhone Settings → Game Center). Scores are submitted when a game ends; if you were offline, the game retries automatically later.

Ad privacy choices

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can change your ad consent in the game under Settings → Ad Privacy Settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.
