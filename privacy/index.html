Blox Fever Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the Blox Fever iOS game (“the App”) uses, why, and the choices you have. The App is developed and published by Egemen Sag, an individual developer (“I”, “me”).

Short version: I don’t run any servers and I don’t collect your personal information. Your game progress stays on your device. The App shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may process device and usage data to serve those ads, subject to your consent where required by law. Game Center and in-app purchases are handled by Apple.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores your current game, scores, local high-score list, and settings (music, sound, haptics) on your device only. This data is not sent to me. Deleting the App deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show:

  • occasional full-screen ads between games, and
  • optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for Board Rescue or Undo.

To deliver, measure, and protect ads against fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device’s IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, advertising-related identifiers available on your device, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. Google’s use of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The App does not ask for permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites (Apple’s App Tracking Transparency), so the App does not access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA).

3. Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Ad Privacy Settings (shown when it applies to your region). If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.

4. Apple Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your final scores are submitted to Apple’s Game Center leaderboard together with your Game Center player profile, so they can appear on global and friends leaderboards. This is processed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can manage Game Center in your device’s Settings.

5. In-app purchase (Remove Ads)

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. I never receive your payment details. The App only checks Apple’s verified purchase record to know whether automatic ads should be removed.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact me and I will help.

7. Data retention and your rights

Because I do not collect or store your personal information, I have no personal data to access, correct, or delete. Data held by Google or Apple is retained under their own policies, and you can exercise your rights directly with them using the links above. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have additional rights; you can contact me with any request or question.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
